How effects work

Four controls shape every effect

Speed
How many times a second the pattern repeats. 1.0 is one cycle a second, or 60 BPM. Flashing patterns are capped at 3 a second in the preview, for safety.
Level / size
How far the effect pushes each light: brightness for dimmer effects, degrees of travel for movement, colour strength for colour, and beam change for zoom.
Phase spread
How far apart the lights are in the pattern. At 0° every light moves together; at 360° the pattern travels once across the whole group, like a wave.
Direction
The order the pattern travels: left to right, right to left, from the centre out, from the outside in, or a random order.
Running an effect on the stage
  1. Save the effect here (signed in as admin).
  2. Open the console, sign in, and click the effect in the Effects pool to start or stop it.
  3. Press Store to save the running effects with the cue. Going to that cue starts them again.
